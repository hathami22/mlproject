Temporal Trajectory Clustering

Predict the performance category of an academy based on temporal metrics

About This Model

This model analyzes French Baccalaureat academy data (2021-2024) and classifies academies into 3 performance categories:

  • Large Metropolitan: Large urban academies (~90% success, ~42,000 students)
  • Emerging Territories: Developing regions (~76% success, rapid growth)
  • Stable High-Performers: Mature academies (~92% success, stable growth)
